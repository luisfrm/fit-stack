description: Captures a new requirement as a Linear issue and hands it to the planner
---

Capture the following as a Linear issue:

$ARGUMENTS

1. **Check first.** Search Linear for the scope. If an open issue already covers it, use that one; if a closed one does, report it and stop.
2. **Create the issue** in team `Rivas Digital`: a title that states the problem rather than the solution, state `Backlog`, a priority, and one label — `Feature`, `Bug` or `Improvement`.
3. **Write what and why** in the description: `## Problem` (2–5 sentences) and `## Scope` with **In** / **Out**. Ask before inventing anything missing.
4. **Hand it to the `planner` agent** to complete the plan, the acceptance criteria, the verification and the risks — and to split it into sub-issues if it is really several outcomes.

Unowned ideas with no owner or scope stay in `vaults/backlog/` and do not become issues on their own.
